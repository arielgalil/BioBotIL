# Initial Concept
BIO-Bot: A biology tutor application using Gemini AI to provide interactive, accurate, and curriculum-aligned explanations in Hebrew.

# Product Guide - BIO-Bot

## Vision & Purpose
BIO-Bot serves as a personal, intelligent biology tutor for Israeli high school students preparing for their matriculation exams (Bagrut), as well as teachers and general biology enthusiasts. The mission is to bridge the gap between complex biological concepts and student understanding through clear, localized, and scientifically rigorous explanations.

## Target Audience
- **High School Students:** Specifically those in Israel studying for the Biology Bagrut.
- **Teachers:** Educators looking for interactive tools to supplement their classroom teaching.
- **Enthusiasts:** Anyone with an interest in biology who wants to learn in a simplified yet accurate manner.

## Core Features
- **Intelligent Streaming Chat:** Real-time, friendly dialogue using a "Tutor" persona powered by Google Gemini.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA):** Fully installable on mobile and desktop with offline support for the application shell.
- **Interactive Educational Widgets:** Dynamic generation of Knowledge Questions (Multiple Choice, True/False) and Application Tasks (Causal Chains, Cloze sentences).
- **Localized Content:** Full support for Hebrew language and Right-to-Left (RTL) interface design.
- **Multi-Part Explanations:** A structured 4-part response system (Opening, Scientific Explanation, Analogy, and Bonus/Trivia) to maximize engagement and retention.
- **Dark Mode Support:** A modern, accessible UI that respects user preferences.

## Educational Strategy
- **Scientific Rigor:** Strict adherence to the Israeli High School Biology Curriculum and scientific consensus.
- **Analogy-Based Learning:** Using everyday comparisons to demystify cellular and physiological processes.
- **High Engagement:** Incorporating "Bonus" content such as surprising facts, riddles, or jokes to maintain student interest.
